# FishQuest 3D FishQuest 3D is an exciting fishing adventure game where you take on the role of a fisherman and explore a beautiful 3D world filled with lakes, islands, mountains, trees, and mysterious fish. Your goal is simple: catch as many fish as possible, build your collection, earn money, and become the ultimate fishing master. Start by casting your fishing rod into the water and waiting for a fish to bite. When a fish gets on your hook, the real challenge begins. You will have 30 seconds to click a moving target as many times as possible. The target moves around the screen, making it harder to hit. Every successful click increases your tap count. The better you perform, the higher your chances of catching a rare fish. Your number of taps determines the rarity of your catch. Four or fewer taps can result in a Common fish, while five to nine taps can give you an Uncommon fish. Ten to fourteen taps can earn you a Rare fish, fifteen to nineteen can give you an Epic fish, and twenty to twenty-nine can give you a Mythic fish. If you manage to reach 30 or more taps, you can catch a Legendary fish. There are 99 different fish to discover, including Goldfish, Salmon, Tuna, Swordfish, Great White Sharks, Megalodon, Golden Koi, Leviathan Fish, and many more. Every fish has its own rarity and value. After catching a fish, you have two choices: keep it or sell it. Kept fish are added to your Fish Chart, where you can see every fish you have discovered and how many you currently own. You can also sell your stored fish whenever you want to earn more money. Use your money in the Fishing Shop to purchase upgrades. You can make the moving target bigger, slow down its movement, or increase the amount of time you have during the fishing challenge. Can you discover all 99 fish, earn a fortune, upgrade your equipment, and become the greatest fisherman in FishQuest 3D?
